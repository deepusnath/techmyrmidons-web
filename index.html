Pick a domain. Follow its Myrmidon.

A Techmyrmidon is a virtual technology icon that keeps itself current, changes its tools to match the trends, and follows the right experts in one field. Follow the Myrmidon for your area and you get four things a search engine answers badly: what changed, what evidence supports it, where you stand, and what to learn next.

No account needed. Nothing is uploaded — what you mark stays in this browser.
