## How we got here: 2022 → 2026

- **2022:** The great rethink: hybrid work models settle in; retention becomes strategy, not slogan
- **2023:** ChatGPT lands on every HR desk; drafting, screening, and policy work change quietly and fast
- **2024:** Skills-based hiring crosses from talk to practice at scale; degree requirements start dropping from major-employer postings
- **2025:** AI copilots automate HR operations ("vibe coding" reaches the people function: HR Evolve ran exactly this session, July 2025); AI-screening bias becomes a regulatory topic
- **2026:** The frontier question: what does talent mean when every employee has an AI counterpart, and how do you verify skill when credentials and AI-polished applications both inflate

## Adopt

| Tool / practice | Why | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| Skills-based hiring frameworks | Hire for demonstrated capability, not degree proxies; the data on wider talent pools is consistent | [ai-seeded] |

## Hold

| Tool / practice | Why | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| Keyword-matching ATS as the primary filter | Rejects the capable, rewards the keyword-stuffed, and AI-written CVs broke it completely | [ai-seeded] |
| Degree as the default proxy for ability | The signal that skills-based hiring exists to replace | [ai-seeded] |
| Annual-only performance reviews | Feedback delayed a year is archaeology, not management | [ai-seeded] |
| Surveillance-style productivity monitoring | Destroys the trust it claims to measure | [ai-seeded] |
