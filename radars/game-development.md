## How we got here: 2022 → 2026

- **2022:** The NFT/play-to-earn wave crashes; survivors relearn that games must first be fun
- **2023:** Unity's pricing debacle triggers a genuine exodus; Godot 4 arrives at the right moment; Unreal 5's Nanite/Lumen reset visual expectations
- **2024:** AI-assisted asset pipelines (concept art, texture, audio drafts) move from controversy to workflow, with credit-and-consent debates unresolved
- **2025:** Small-team renaissance: AI tooling collapses production costs; discoverability, not production, becomes the bottleneck
- **2026:** Generative NPCs and dynamic dialogue ship in real titles; design leadership matters more than headcount
